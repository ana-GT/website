I am a robotics researcher at TRACLabs, in Houston, Texas. My research interests focus on generalizable robot manipulation. I investigate how to enable robots to grasp stuff around them. Check out the Research section of my page for more information. During my grad school years, I was advised by Prof. Henrik Christensen and by Prof. Mike Stilman).
